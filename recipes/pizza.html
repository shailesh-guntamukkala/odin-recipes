PIZZA

Description

Pizza is a savory dish of Italian origin, consisting of a usually round crust topped with sauce, cheese, and various toppings. It is baked in an oven and is a popular food item worldwide.

Pizza is made by preparing a dough base, which is then topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and a variety of ingredients such as vegetables, meats, and herbs. The pizza is then baked until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly. It can be served hot or cold and is often enjoyed as a casual meal or snack.
