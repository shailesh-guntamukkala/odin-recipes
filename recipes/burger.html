Burger

Description

A burger is a type of sandwich that consists of one or more meat patties, typically beef, placed inside a sliced bread roll. The patty is often served with various toppings such as lettuce, tomato, onion, and condiments like ketchup, mustard, and mayonnaise.

Ingredients

  • 1 hamburger bun
  • 1 ground beef patty
  • 1 leaf of lettuce
  • 1 tomato slice
  • 1 onion slice
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon mayonnaise

Steps

  1. Preheat the grill or skillet to medium-high heat.
  2. Form the ground beef into a patty and place it on the grill or skillet.
  3. Cook the patty for about 4-5 minutes on each side, or until fully cooked.
  4. While the patty is cooking, toast the hamburger bun halves.
  5. Spread ketchup on one half of the bun.
  6. Place the cooked patty on top of the ketchup.
  7. Add lettuce, tomato, and onion slices.
  8. Spread mustard and mayonnaise on the other half of the bun.
  9. Closed the burger with the top half of the bun.
